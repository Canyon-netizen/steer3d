Intervention algorithm guide：下一步怎么设计

把当前证据映射到向量与干预算法的选择；区分已验证、未验证与失败的方向。

决策当前证据建议
目标定义marker 读出 vs correctness 效应是不同目标；+v 对 correctness 为负、对 7196 为负。先固定 target；不要用 w·U 的 marker 可读性代替 correctness。
读出层per-marker best layer 17/21/27；set-level 21 是反相关。读出层用于诊断，不要直接当注入层；注入层当前固定 block 20。
方向mean g 在 held-out 上最好；7196 sparse g@0.02；10061 mean g@0.02；6771 w@0.1。per-marker 方向；7196 方向对其它 marker 为负，不能共享。
剂量KL≤0.1 窗口；mean g 效果大但 KL 大；sparse g 更省。按 KL 预算选 dose，不选最大 Δlogp。
干预算法one-shot fixed recipe 已验证；per-token 梯度控制器未测；unit block/restore 的 naive 修复失败。先部署 one-shot；per-token 控制器作为下一实验；unit 级需要精确 ∇_h a_i。
验证当前 held-out 与 verification 共用 site；没有任务级指标。独立第三 split + 任务级副作用 + 第二模型后再升级。

算法选项

算法证据/状态下一步
A. One-shot fixed vector（当前）compiled recipe：3 个 marker，held-out 验证，KL 预算内；成本最低。直接用于离线前缀干预；换前缀/层/模型必须重验。
B. Per-token gradient controller未测；每 token 需一次 backward，成本高；可能跟踪 target 更好，也可能漂移。下一实验：固定前缀生成中每步注入 λg_t，比较 Δlogp/KL/生成质量。
C. Unit-level block/restore原生单元分解是诊断；naive write-direction 移除失败。先算精确 ∇_h a_i，再做 unit patch/block；不要用 d_i 投影代替。
D. Sparse / low-rank directionsparse g 在相同 dose 下 KL 更低，保留大部分 Δlogp。候选；在独立 split 上比较 sparse vs dense 的 KL/任务副作用。

推荐的下一批实验

  1. 独立第三 held-out split（当前 verification 复用 transfer/sweep site）。
  2. 任务级副作用：正确率、其它 marker 的 per-marker 概率、生成质量，而不是只有 KL。
  3. Per-token controller 与 one-shot recipe 的对照实验。
  4. 正则化方向（clip / low-rank / unit-sparse）在相同 Δlogp 下比较 KL。
  5. 第二模型/第二向量；把选窗口规则预登记。

recommendations

high · define_target
The vector is readable for the marker set but its measured causal effect is on multiplication correctness, and that effect is negative at the primary dose. Designing from w·U alone would optimise the wrong readout.
  • freq-weighted w·U = +0.18424, 7/7 marker ids positive
  • +v primary effect on correctness score = -0.06641
high · avoid_positive_injection
Block-20 units move with the vector's net (negative) effect: restoring them during +v lowers the score further, and restoring them into baseline lowers it too. Injecting +v at this layer pushes the measured readout the wrong way for a correctness target.
  • block -0.04967, restore -0.04533, support +0.04750
  • net +v effect at dose 0.1 = -0.06641
high · test_compensator_removal
Block-25 units oppose the vector's net effect: restoring them during +v raises the score, and restoring them into baseline also raises it. This is the first layer to test for removal / sign flip before building a new direction.
  • block +0.08236, restore +0.08939, compensation +0.08588
  • same-sign block/restore = True; random rank 2/5
low · do_not_use_as_lever
These layers have no layer-specific block/restore effect above the floor; do not present them as a mechanism without new measurements.
  • weak layers: 22, 23, 26, 27
high · choose_direction_by_sign
The effect reverses with the sign of the injected direction and grows with dose. For a correctness target the current +v is the wrong sign at the primary dose; -v is positive at every tested dose.
  • w+ primary -0.06641; w- primary +0.10387; random primary -0.02005
  • w+ at dose 0.02: -0.01600; w- at dose 0.02: +0.01768
medium · condition_on_wording
The sign of the effect depends on wording and equation truth. Fix the target wording before comparing vectors or doses.
  • correct/neutral +0.10610; correct/self-check -0.25971; incorrect/neutral -0.13528; incorrect/self-check +0.02324
high · do_not_treat_marker_set_as_one_concept
Readout is positive for all markers, but the injection effect depends on which marker token is present. The dominant marker is suppressed by +w in the original marker-steering report, so the set average is not a single control target.
  • dominant marker id 7196: w·U +0.17920, share 0.251
  • marker-steering report: +w suppresses the dominant marker while raising the others
high · do_not_use_cos_h_w_as_positive_readout
The hidden-state atlas finds its best marker separation at layer 21, but the orientation is negative: marker next-token positions have LOWER cos(h,w) (raw AUC 0.1991; oriented AUC 0.8009). This holds in 24/24 trajectories with both classes. The static w·U readout therefore does not transfer to a positive state-space projection; do not select layers or units by w·h.
  • best layer 21: raw cos AUC 0.1991, oriented cos AUC 0.8009, orientation -1
  • norm control AUC 0.5632; residualised raw AUC 0.1927
  • n_marker=384, n_trajectories=30
high · split_marker_directions_by_sign
Per-marker readout resolves the set-level anti-alignment: marker 7196 (n=105) is positively oriented at layer 17 (raw cos AUC 0.7305), while marker 13824 is the strongest negative (n=34, layer 27, raw cos AUC 0.0725). The set-level AUC hides this sign split. A single direction/sign cannot positively read all seven; pick the target marker and build its own direction and layer.
  • marker 6771: n=119, best layer 27, raw cos AUC 0.1453, orientation -1
  • marker 7196: n=105, best layer 17, raw cos AUC 0.7305, orientation 1
  • marker 10061: n=70, best layer 21, raw cos AUC 0.1620, orientation -1
  • marker 14190: n=46, best layer 19, raw cos AUC 0.3690, orientation -1
  • marker 13824: n=34, best layer 27, raw cos AUC 0.0725, orientation -1
  • marker 88190: n=6, best layer 18, raw cos AUC 0.0297, orientation -1
  • marker 80022: n=4, best layer 9, raw cos AUC 0.0197, orientation -1
low · do_not_adjudicate_small_n_markers
These markers have fewer than 10 occurrences in the 30-trajectory atlas; their per-marker AUC is not adjudicable and must not be used to choose a direction.
  • small-n markers: 88190, 80022
high · use_gradient_direction_as_constructive_baseline
On 6 short AIME prefixes, the normalized local gradient g = d log p(marker) / d h at layer 20 improved the target on every site and was the best candidate for all tested markers; w was negative for 7196 and weaker for 10061/6771, and w+g matched g. Use g as the constructive baseline, then audit native units.
  • marker 10061: mean delta w +0.12541 (improved 1.00), gradient +0.36427 (improved 1.00), n=2
  • marker 6771: mean delta w +0.12154 (improved 1.00), gradient +0.34194 (improved 1.00), n=2
  • marker 7196: mean delta w -0.00828 (improved 0.50), gradient +0.01589 (improved 1.00), n=2
medium · treat_unit_decomposition_as_diagnostic
The per-unit decomposition is first-order and its sum-to-measured ratio varies across sites; the quadrants are useful for diagnosing carriers vs compensators, but they are not a validated mediation percentage, and naive removal of write-direction components failed in the prototype.
  • see experiments/steering_construction_v1/steering_construction.json decomposition.ratio
  • gradient is a white-box local direction; it is not a semantically named steering vector
